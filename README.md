🌟 ABOUT THE PLATFORM

We are building a next-generation digital platform focused on gaming, entertainment, streaming, and creator content. Our goal is to provide creators with the tools they need to share their content, build an audience, interact with their community, and eventually turn their creativity into opportunities.

Users will be able to discover gaming content, watch videos, follow creators, participate in communities, and experience a platform built around modern digital entertainment.

For creators, the platform is designed to provide a place where they can upload videos 🎥, start livestreams 🔴, build their profiles 👤, interact with viewers 💬, and grow their audience 📈.

🐛 BUG REPORTING

Found a bug or unexpected behavior?

Please report it with as much information as possible.

A useful bug report should include:

• 📝 Clear description of the issue
• 📱 Device and operating system
• 🌐 Browser or platform used
• 🔢 App/website version, if available
• 🔄 Steps to reproduce the problem
• 🎯 Expected behavior
• ❌ Actual behavior
• 📸 Screenshot or screen recording when possible

Detailed reports help our development team identify and fix problems faster.
